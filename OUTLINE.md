# The Polycrisis Apocalypse Humanity Is Facing — OUTLINE (word-budgeted)

**Claim (one line):** Humanity is not heading for a sudden apocalypse but for a centuries-long polycrisis apocalypse — a demographic and environmental re-sorting that traps the majority of the species inside survivability risk regions, stabilizes it at unsafe levels, and only then decays toward extinction — unless the compounding loops are broken now.

## Method note (honesty constraint)

The report treats "evolutionary progression," "average human is less developed by tens of thousands of years," and "diaspora dominance → utter collapse" as the author's scenario hypotheses to be tested — not as established facts. Where the evidence contradicts the hypothesis (human evolution is not a single ladder; diasporas typically transmit skills and capital), the report says so and reframes the risk in measurable terms: institutional fragility, human-capital gaps, and compounding hazard exposure in the risk regions where the future median human will live.
